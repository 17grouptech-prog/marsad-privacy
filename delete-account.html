In English

Fastest — from inside the app

Open Marsad → Settings → Account → Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It needs no approval from us and no waiting.

If you have already uninstalled the app

Email 17grouptech@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Account deletion request”.

We verify the request and delete the account within 7 business days at the latest, and reply to confirm once it is done. You do not need to reinstall the app or sign in to make the request.

What is deleted

  • Every transaction you recorded
  • Your accounts and budgets
  • Your income sources and subscriptions
  • Your custom categories
  • Your email address and account identifier
  • The account itself

What remains, and for how long

Receipt photos are never uploaded to our servers. They stay on your device and are removed with it when you delete your account from inside the app, or when you uninstall.

Encrypted backups expire on their own within 30 days of deletion. After that there is no trace left.

Purchase records held by Google Play are not ours and we cannot delete them. For those, see your Google Play account settings.

Before you delete

If you want to keep your records, export them as CSV from Settings → Export data. Export works with or without an active subscription, and after deletion there is no way back.
